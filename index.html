France traditions

  • Bastille Day — Celebrated on July 14 with fireworks, parades, music, and parties.
  • La Chandeleur — A French holiday celebrated on February 2 where people traditionally make and eat crêpes with family and friends.
  • Christmas Markets that have festive decorations, local foods, handmade gifts, and holiday activities.
