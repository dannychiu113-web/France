The "Why": Purpose of This Site

The goal of this website is to inspire travelers, outdoor enthusiasts, and students to discover and appreciate the timeless wonder of Yosemite National Park. Located in California's Sierra Nevada mountains, Yosemite spans nearly 1,200 square miles of deep valleys, alpine meadows, ancient giant sequoias, and world-famous granite cliffs.

Through this site, visitors can explore the park's rich history, understand its geological features, and gain essential research-backed knowledge to appreciate one of America's greatest natural treasures.
