The History of Yosemite

A Quick Look at Yosemite's Past

Timeline of Yosemite

1. Early Native Heritage

For thousands of years, the Ahwahnechee people lived in Yosemite Valley. They called the area "Ahwahnee" and cared for the land by using controlled fires to clear brush and grow oak trees for acorns.

2. The Gold Rush Era (1850s)

During the 1849 California Gold Rush, thousands of miners moved into the region. This led to conflict and forced the native inhabitants out of the valley.

3. The Yosemite Grant (1864)

To protect the valley's beauty from being destroyed by development, President Abraham Lincoln signed the Yosemite Grant in 1864. This set aside the land for public preservation for the first time in US history.

4. Becoming a National Park (1890)

Thanks to efforts by famous nature writer John Muir, Congress officially made Yosemite a National Park on October 1, 1890, protecting over 750,000 acres of wilderness.
