3 Key Facts & Statistics

  • Most Visited Country: France gets about 90 million international tourists every year, making it the #1 most visited nation in the world.
  • Over 400 Cheeses: France officially makes over 400 to 1,000 different types of cheeses across its regions.
  • Huge Art Museum: If you spent 30 seconds looking at every item in Paris's Louvre Museum, it would take you 100 days to finish.
