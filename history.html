Origin & Ancient Times

France was originally called Gaul and was lived in by Celtic tribes. Julius Caesar and the Roman army conquered the area in 51 BC, bringing Latin culture and building early roads and cities.

The French Revolution

  • It inspired ideas about freedom, equality, and citizenship
  • Its values still influence modern France and Europe

Why It Matters
