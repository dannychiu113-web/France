Origin & Ancient Times

France was originally called Gaul and was lived in by Celtic tribes. Julius Caesar and the Roman army conquered the area in 51 BC, bringing Latin culture and building early roads and cities.

Timeline of Key Events

Year Event Impact
51 BC Roman conquest of Gaul Spread Latin culture and roads
843 Treaty of Verdun Helped shape medieval France
1789 French Revolution begins Changed government and ideas of rights

The French Revolution

  • It began in 1789 and changed the monarchy's power
  • It inspired ideas about freedom, equality, and citizenship
  • Its values still influence modern France and Europe

Why It Matters

  • France's history shaped its culture and identity
  • It helped define many modern political ideas
  • It explains why France remains important in world history
