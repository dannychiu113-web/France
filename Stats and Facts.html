Quick Facts & Stats

Fast highlights about Yosemite National Park

3 Key Highlights

1. Huge Waterfalls

Yosemite Falls drops 2,425 feet, making it the tallest waterfall in North America.

2. Ancient Trees

The Grizzly Giant sequoia tree is over 3,000 years old and over 96 feet wide at the bottom.

3. Giant Rock Wall

El Capitan is a solid granite cliff that stands 3,000 feet straight up from the ground.
